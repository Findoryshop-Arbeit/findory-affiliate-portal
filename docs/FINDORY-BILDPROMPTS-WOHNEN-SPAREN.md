# Findory Bildprompts – Wohnen & Sparen

Die Bilder sind ruhige, realistische Querformate für die Landingpage-Karten. Keine Schrift, keine Logos, keine Wasserzeichen.

## Heizkosten senken

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Findory Landingpage-Karte, 3:2 Querformat
Primary request: a bright, lived-in German apartment living room with a clean white radiator and a clearly visible modern thermostat valve, a folded wool blanket nearby, energy-conscious but warm atmosphere
Style/medium: premium editorial interior photography, realistic
Composition/framing: wide horizontal crop, radiator and thermostat clearly visible as the visual focus, uncluttered negative space
Lighting/mood: soft natural winter daylight, calm and reassuring
Constraints: no people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no exaggerated luxury interior
```
